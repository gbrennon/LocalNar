# March of the Black Monolith

The song the repository name answers to. Written for **Black Cascade**, a
Brazilian black metal band, and sung from the Loc-Nar's side of the story: the
same orb the film *Heavy Metal* (1981) built its anthology around, here given
the first-person treatment. The bracket in "Unknow[Loc-nar] is my name" is the
lyric's own reveal - `Unknow[n]` with the missing piece named.

```text
Incarnated in hatred

```

## Where the lines land

- **"I am the sum of all evil"** - the orb's own job description in the film;
  the README tagline quotes it.
- **"Searching for some worlds to crack"** and **"I'm the one who can distort
  every world/one"** - the anthology itself: each segment is another world the
  orb cracks open.
- **"I am the one who hold the name"** and **"Unknow[Loc-nar] is my name"** -
  the name reveal the repository borrows: `LocalNar` is `local` welded onto
  the Loc-Nar.
- **"My power infests all the times and galaxies"** - the film's arc: the orb
  surfaces in every era it visits, from a wartime bomber to a space station to
  Taarna's world.
- **"Cutting through the tissue of reality / Changing my form so that i can
  infiltrate into your lives"** - the orb's shape-shifting between segments;
  the same move a model file makes across its quantizations.
- **"I demand your power" ... "those who have lost their time will speak"** -
  the coveters, in the film and everywhere: whoever summons the orb wants its
  power and loses their time to it.
- **"Looking over the behaviour of living things / You are beneath my will /
  There's no way to escape from my presence"** - the orb over its servants;
  also a fair description of a multi-gigabyte model directory you stopped
  auditing.
- **"I am nothing but a simple existence"** - the closing irony the manager
  takes seriously: under it all, the thing is just bytes on your own disk, and
  bytes you can prove.

The film's last word on the orb is its defender, Taarna, who struck only what
had earned it. This repository's `p` works the same way.
